# Threecommerce

**Magento 2 / Mage-OS modules, built and maintained in Italy.**
Moduli per Magento 2 e Mage-OS, sviluppati e mantenuti in Italia. Compatibili con temi **Hyvä**, **Breeze** e Luma, **PHP 8.4**.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-threecommerce.it-3e287c)](https://threecommerce.it/moduli?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile)

## What we build

- **Checkout & payments**: one-step checkout (Hyvä and Breeze), Banca Sella / Axerve
- **Italian e-commerce compliance**: electronic invoicing fields (CF, P.IVA, SDI/PEC), Fattura24, cookie banner with consent log, GDPR newsletter consent
- **Shipping**: store pickup, store locator (Google Maps or OpenStreetMap), Poste Delivery Business, free-shipping progress bar
- **Catalog & storefront**: product labels, catalog mode, menu per store view, out-of-stock sorting, slider
- **SEO & analytics**: JSON-LD, canonical, hreflang, GA4 behind cookie consent
- **Admin**: reports, maintenance mode, user time zone

Free and paid modules, each with a full product page, user guide and changelog:
